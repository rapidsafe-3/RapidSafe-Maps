About

Building Honest, Affordable Location Infrastructure

RapidSafe Maps exists because location intelligence shouldn't come with billing anxiety attached.

Our Mission

Too many developers discover mapping costs the hard way — after their app gains traction and the invoices start climbing. RapidSafe Maps was built to remove that anxiety with flat, predictable pricing and a drop-in migration path.

Location intelligence shouldn't require a massive budget just to render a map on a screen.

What We Value

Radical Transparency

No hidden per-request fees. What you see on the pricing page is what you pay.

Developer-First

Every decision is made from the perspective of the person integrating the API.

Built for Scale

A gateway architecture designed to keep median latency under 40ms as usage grows.

Student-Led Innovation

Built by a student developer who felt the billing pain firsthand and decided to fix it.
